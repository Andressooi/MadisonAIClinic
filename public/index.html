How the score is made

We send in the customers who are happy to tell you what’s wrong — and right.

Nobody calls to explain why they hired someone else. They just hire someone else, and you never learn a thing. So we build the customers who will talk — and then we check every word they say.

  • Marcy, 58 Water in the basement, 9:40 on a Sunday night

    “I found three plumbers on my phone. Two had a number I could tap. Yours had a form that wanted my address. I called the second one.”

  • Terri, 44 Property manager, taking three bids on an eight-unit building

    “Nothing on the site mentioned commercial work. I assumed residential only and never got as far as the contact page.”

  • Sam, 31 Price-checking before calling anybody

    “Two of the four I looked at post a flat price to clear a drain. You say call for a quote. I called the ones with a number on the page.”

To be plain about it

Marcy, Terri and Sam are AI, not people. Each one is sent through your business the way that buyer would actually go, and nothing any of them reports reaches your report until it has been checked against your real website, your real listings and real competitors. A feeling is not a finding until somebody can point at the cause.

And they are not made up

The profiles are built from public data about your actual service area — Census and American Community Survey figures for who lives in those ZIP codes, their ages, households, tenure and income; local search behaviour for your trade; and the language people use in the reviews of businesses like yours. Marcy is 58 and owns her house because that is the middle of the homeowner distribution where you work, not because it sounded about right. When your market changes, the panel changes with it.

Then I read all of it and decide what actually matters. The machines gather. The judgment is mine.

We do not rank you against everyone with a listing. We pick a handful of companies worth learning from. Some are down the road. Some are three states away and will never take a job from you — which is exactly why they are the safest to copy.
